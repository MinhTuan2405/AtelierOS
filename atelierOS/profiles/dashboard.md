# Dashboard Profile

## Purpose

Operational, monitoring, reporting, and analytical interfaces where users scan,
compare, diagnose, and act on real information.

## Primary Priorities

- usability and information density
- navigation and orientation
- data readability and truthful representation
- state coverage and actionable feedback
- consistency across repeated modules

## Recommended Engine Balance

Impeccable is the primary engine. Taste is secondary and should be used only for
selective visual character that does not weaken comprehension.

## Layout Behavior

Organize by decisions and tasks, not by equal-sized card grids. Keep filters,
time ranges, units, legends, and data provenance close to what they affect.
Choose an explicit narrow-screen strategy for tables and dense visualizations.

## Density

Moderate to high, calibrated to task frequency and user expertise. Avoid both
cramped controls and marketing-page whitespace.

## Motion

Minimal and functional: state transitions, changed data, loading, and drill-down
context. Never animate data in a way that impairs comparison.

## Typography Direction

Compact, stable UI typography with tabular numerals where useful. Hierarchy must
separate page, section, metric, unit, label, and annotation clearly.

## Common Risks

- fake or decorative metrics
- chart variety without analytical purpose
- card soup and low information-to-chrome ratio
- missing loading, stale, empty, error, or partial-data states
- treating dashboards like marketing pages

## Review Focus

Review data meaning, scanning order, task completion, filters and states,
responsive data handling, accessibility of charts, and density consistency.
