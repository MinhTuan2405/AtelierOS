# Ecommerce Profile

## Purpose

Discovery, evaluation, purchase, and post-purchase surfaces where clarity,
confidence, and transaction integrity are essential.

## Primary Priorities

- product comprehension and findability
- price, availability, delivery, and policy clarity
- trustworthy conversion flow
- variant, inventory, cart, validation, and payment states
- mobile purchase usability

## Recommended Engine Balance

Impeccable leads flow structure, state design, and refinement. Taste contributes
brand-led merchandising, campaigns, and editorial product storytelling.

## Layout Behavior

Keep product evidence close to decisions. Maintain persistent context through
search, filtering, variants, cart, and checkout. Do not let editorial expression
separate critical details from the purchase action.

## Density

Moderate. Listing pages may be dense for comparison; detail and checkout pages
should group decisions clearly without excessive container chrome.

## Motion

Use restrained feedback for galleries, variants, cart changes, and validation.
Never delay or obscure transaction state.

## Typography Direction

Readable and confidence-building, with unambiguous prices, units, discounts,
availability, and policy details. Brand typography must not impair product data.

## Common Risks

- hidden fees or unclear price hierarchy
- inaccessible galleries and option selectors
- invented urgency or decorative trust badges
- missing edge states for variants, inventory, and payment
- mobile controls displaced by imagery

## Review Focus

Review product discovery, decision information, transaction states, trust,
keyboard and touch use, mobile checkout, and truthful merchandising.
