# Editorial Profile

## Purpose

Publications, articles, essays, documentation, and content-led experiences where
reading, discovery, and context are primary.

## Primary Priorities

- reading comfort and content hierarchy
- editorial voice and typographic character
- navigation, wayfinding, and metadata
- media, captions, citations, and related context
- durable responsive rhythm

## Recommended Engine Balance

Taste may lead typographic and compositional expression. Impeccable leads
information architecture, reading flow, interaction, and refinement.

## Layout Behavior

Use a stable reading column with intentional interruptions for media, notes, or
related material. Separate publication navigation from article hierarchy and
make long-form orientation clear.

## Density

Content-led and measured. Indexes can be dense; article bodies need comfortable
measure, rhythm, and distinction between primary and supporting material.

## Motion

Minimal by default. Use it for orientation, disclosure, or meaningful media,
never as a prerequisite for reading.

## Typography Direction

Build a complete hierarchy for title, deck, byline, metadata, body, subheads,
quotes, captions, notes, and code or data where present. Validate real content.

## Common Risks

- expressive layouts that break reading order
- weak contrast between content levels
- overly wide measure or tiny metadata
- decorative pull quotes that repeat rather than enrich content
- mobile layouts that lose notes, captions, or context

## Review Focus

Review reading order, measure and rhythm, navigation, semantics, media context,
long-content responsiveness, zoom, and typography across actual content types.
