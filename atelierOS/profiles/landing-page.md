# Landing Page Profile

## Purpose

Marketing pages that explain an offering, establish trust, and move a defined
audience toward a conversion action.

## Primary Priorities

- strong, product-specific art direction
- clear conversion hierarchy and evidence
- differentiated narrative pacing
- responsive storytelling
- fast comprehension and credible claims

## Recommended Engine Balance

Taste is the primary visual engine. Impeccable leads structural shaping,
responsive coherence, and final refinement.

## Layout Behavior

Build a deliberate sequence around audience questions rather than repeating a
section template. Vary composition only to reinforce the narrative. Keep the
primary message and action visible at practical laptop and phone sizes.

## Density

Moderate and paced. Sparse moments need contrast from evidence-rich sections;
do not use empty space to compensate for missing content.

## Motion

Use motion to reveal relationships, demonstrate the product, or control pacing.
Avoid ambient motion that competes with conversion and respect reduced motion.

## Typography Direction

Use expressive display typography with a highly readable text companion. Favor
decisive scale contrast, controlled line length, and product-specific tone.

## Common Risks

- generic SaaS composition and interchangeable copy
- unsupported claims, decorative metrics, and fake product UI
- repeated three-column cards
- oversized heroes that defer evidence
- visual novelty that obscures the offering

## Review Focus

Check first-screen comprehension, conversion order, trust, differentiation,
mobile narrative, performance-sensitive media, and anti-patterns.
